I am a PhD candidate in Sociology at UC Berkeley. Broadly, my work is at the intersection of economic sociology, organizational strategy, and climate governance. I research how firms reconcile accountability in nonfinancial reporting with sustaining competitive advantage. Specifically, through the case of corporate sustainability, I study emergent organizational strategies for maintaining business performance alongside social and environmental outcomes.
